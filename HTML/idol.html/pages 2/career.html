My Career

My professional journey began far from the bright lights of the Premier League. At just 17 years old, I made the bold, independent choice to leave my home in Maia, Portugal, for Italy. Joining Serie B side Novara on a meager salary, I was forced to grow up quickly both as a man and a footballer. This period, followed by top-flight Serie A stints at Udinese and Sampdoria, served as my footballing crucible. Italy taught me the tactical discipline, defensive work rate, and mental toughness that still define my game today. I learned how to survive in tight spaces and read the game a split-second ahead of everyone else.Returning to Portugal to join Sporting CP changed my career trajectory entirely. Unshackled from the rigid defensive systems of Italy, I was given the freedom to create, attack, and lead. As captain, I evolved into a prolific, goal-scoring midfielder, guiding the club to two Portuguese League Cups and a Taça de Portugal. Winning back-to-back Primeira Liga Player of the Year awards proved that I had grown out of my prospect status and was ready to command the biggest stages in world football.In January 2020, I completed a dream move to Manchester United. I knew the weight that came with the shirt, and I welcomed the pressure immediately. My impact was instant; I aimed to bring leadership, vision, and a fierce, uncompromising demand for excellence back to the club. Appointed official club captain in July 2023, I have proudly led the squad to domestic silverware, including the 2023 Carabao Cup and the 2024 FA Cup.My career reached a historic individual pinnacle during the recent 2025/26 campaign, where I broke the all-time Premier League single-season record with 21 assists, earning the FWA Footballer of the Year award. Alongside my duties in Manchester, I remain a proud veteran and pillar for the Portugal national team on the world stage. Driven by a hatred of losing and backed by a legendary injury-free fitness record, I continue to play every single match with the same hunger I had as a boy in Maia.
