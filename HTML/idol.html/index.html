Bruno Miguel Borges Fernandes



Early life

I am Bruno Fernandes was born on September 8, 1994, in Maia, Portugal. I grew up playing street football alongside of my older brother. Originally a center-back, I began my youth career at local club Infesta before joining the Boavista FC academy, where he transitioned into an attacking midfielder.

Road to Professional

My parents were Virginia Borges and Jose Fernandes. When I was in the Boavista youth set-up, my father moved to Switzerland for work. My father wanted the family to move with him, but I convinced my parents to let me stay in Portugal to pursue football.The "No" to Switzerland: After refusing to move to Switzerland, I often spent time focusing on playing in my local district of Gueifães, sometimes getting punished by my mother for neglecting academics in favor of football.Early Development: I played the majority of my youth football at Boavista and had a short loan spell at Pasteleira. It was during this loan at age 15 that coaches officially transitioned me from center-back to an attacking midfielder.Move to Italy: In mid-2012, at age 17, I was scouted during an Under-19 fixture and signed for Italian Serie B side Novara.
